# Continuity — Cross-Unit Consistency

Keep names, timelines, references, and prerequisites coherent across the whole
work. A reader who notices a contradiction loses trust in every claim after it.

## Process

1. **Index** — build a single glossary of terms, entities, and references from
   all units.
2. **Check** — each usage must match the glossary (spelling, definition,
   timeline).
3. **Resolve** — when a conflict appears, pick the canonical form and update
   *all* occurrences, not just the one you spotted.

## Anti-Rationalization

- "Close enough" → in continuity, close is wrong. Consistency is binary.
- "Readers won't notice" → one will, and they will distrust the rest.

## Evidence

- Continuity holds when: every indexed term resolves to exactly one definition
  and the timeline/order is internally consistent end to end.

## Red Flags

- Two spellings or definitions for the same entity.
- A reference to something not yet introduced (prerequisite gap).
- Fixing a contradiction in one place while leaving its twin elsewhere.
